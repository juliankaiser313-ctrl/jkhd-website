// System

How a system tries to make money.

A trading program always carries an assumption about why a price moves. Eight basic kinds, each with its catch. Scroll.

01 · Arbitrage

The same thing at two prices.

Buy cheaply there, sell dearly here, in the same moment. The catch: the gap is gone in milliseconds — without the shortest line you arrive too late.

02 · Market making

Earn the spread, not the direction.

Always quote a price to buy and a price to sell. The catch: you get filled in full exactly when everyone runs the same way — and you hold the position nobody wants.

03 · Trend

What moves often keeps moving.

Join the direction that is already running and stay until it breaks. The catch: many small false starts against a few large hits.

04 · Mean reversion

A spike usually comes back.

Trade against the exaggeration and wait. The catch: sometimes the spike is a beginning — then the loss grows with every step you add.

05 · Pairs trading

Two things that normally move together drift apart for a while.

Sell the dearer one, buy the cheaper one, wait for them to meet. The catch: “normally” is an assumption about the past.

06 · Carry

The money comes from waiting, not moving.

Some positions pay you for holding them. The catch: it goes well for a long time and then very badly once — the premium pays for exactly that once.

07 · Events

The price jumps when something new becomes known.

What is traded is the reaction, not your own opinion. The catch: it takes milliseconds, and part of the news is already in the price — whoever reads it trades the second wave.

08 · Order book

Read the next small step from the jostling of orders.

Who wants how much at which price. The catch: that holds for seconds, and orders lie — placed to be seen.
