// Partner access

The area for partners of JKHD.

This is where partners of JKHD sign in. Afterwards your partner page shows what we hold about you: your details, files we have provided for you and — depending on what we have unlocked for you — your access and a dedicated contact address for partners. You only see what belongs to you.

Go to sign-in

// Minimum requirements

What you need for access.

01

You are a partner of JKHD

Your e-mail address is registered with us as a partner. You cannot sign up yourself — we add partners ourselves. Not a partner yet? Write to us below via "Enquiry".

02

Access to your mailbox

We send the sign-in code to this address. It is also how you reset a forgotten password, and where we tell you when something changes about your password.

03

A current browser with JavaScript

On a computer or a phone, for example Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Without JavaScript, sign-in does not work.

Optional: a password of your own. At least ten characters. You can set it on the partner page right after signing in with a code. From then on, e-mail address and password are enough. At least every 180 days we ask you to sign in once with a code.
Partners only

Sign in

"Sign in" asks for your e-mail address. If you have set a password, enter it as well; otherwise leave the field empty and we send you a code. Forgot your password? Then sign in with a code and set a new one. Not a partner yet? "Enquiry" reaches us with a few questions — we confirm receipt by e-mail.
