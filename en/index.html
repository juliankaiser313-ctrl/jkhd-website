What we build

Not every system has to trade. We build the stage you need — and we tell you beforehand which one that is.

Observation

Systems that watch and report: market conditions, data quality, the behaviour of your own strategies. No intervention, no orders — visibility only.

Market access none

Analysis

Systems that compute and evaluate: research pipelines, signal assessment, statistical testing. They deliver results on which your people decide.

Market access none

Execution

Systems that trade: rule set, order management, risk limits and emergency stop. Fully automated — within the limits fixed before the first trade.

Market access fully automated
