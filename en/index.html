// In short

JKHD builds computer programs that buy and sell on the markets by themselves — at fixed times, every trading day, with nobody sitting next to them.

We sell nothing: no tips, no software, no advice. We trade only for ourselves. This page explains in plain words how such a program works and how the company behind it operates. And anyone who is passionate about the same thing is invited to write to us.

// The path

How the program works.

Six steps, from listening in on the markets to the message on the phone. No jargon — and no giving away how exactly we do it.

  1. 1

    Listening

    The program receives the prices from the exchange and writes them down. Everything else is built on these records — not on opinions.

  2. 2

    Looking for patterns

    In those records the program looks for behaviour that repeats: what happens after a fast rise? What after a long quiet spell? It does not try one idea, but thousands of variations of it.

  3. 3

    Sorting out chance

    Almost everything that looks like a pattern is chance. So every idea has to prove that it also works on data it has never seen — and that it does not only look good because we tried so many ideas. Most fail here. That is intended.

  4. 4

    Setting limits

    Before the program is allowed to trade, the rules are set: how much it may lose in a day at most, and how much money it may put into a single trade. Once a limit is reached, it stops — no discussion.

  5. 5

    Trading

    When the program buys, it has already settled at that very moment at what price it will sell again: at which profit and at which loss. That decision is made beforehand — never afterwards, never on gut feeling.

  6. 6

    Keeping watch

    A second program watches the first. If something stalls or behaves unusually, it raises the alarm — and the person behind it gets a message on their phone.

What is missing from this page — on purpose: which patterns the program trades, which numbers it uses and where exactly its limits are. That is our recipe. Like a baker's recipe, it is not on display in the shop window.

// The company

How JKHD works.

Small, independent, beholden to no one.

01

We trade only for ourselves

There are no clients, and we manage nobody's money. Whatever the program makes or loses concerns only us. So we do not have to promise anyone anything — and we don't.

02

One head, one pair of hands

Behind JKHD is one founder who builds, tests and runs everything himself. That keeps the company small — and every decision traceable. Nobody has to guess who is responsible.

03

Most of the time we say no

Most ideas we examine end up in the bin because they fail the chance test. That is not failure, it is the actual job: whoever sorts nothing out is trading chance.
