JKHD develops quantitative trading systems — exclusively for internal use.

An independent firm based in Schliengen, Germany. We design, validate, and operate our systems entirely in-house. No third-party sales or commercial licensing.

Profile

Built Out of Internal Necessity

JKHD develops and operates quantitative trading systems: software architectures that execute decisions based on mathematical and statistical models — fully automated and free from human discretion.

JKHD was not founded on a theoretical business plan, but on a concrete technical requirement: Trading financial markets driven strictly by data rather than intuition requires an infrastructure that commercially available software cannot provide. This includes:

  • Gapless, raw market data without silent retrospective smoothing.
  • Validation frameworks engineered to rigorously reject flawed hypotheses.
  • Automated fail-safe mechanisms that trigger independently of human oversight.

Because off-the-shelf tools fell short of these standards, we built the entire execution and research pipeline from scratch. The result is a proprietary system architecture operating under continuous, monitored conditions.

Core Principles

01

Proprietary System Architecture

Data pipelines, quantitative research, execution software, risk limits, and live operations are created entirely in-house. Rejecting external black-box solutions guarantees complete transparency and control over every line of code.

02

Statistical Significance as a Gatekeeper

Every model must prove its statistical edge over randomness. Discarding a hypothesis is not considered a failure, but a validated research outcome.

03

Hardcoded Risk Boundaries

Drawdown limits and automated emergency shut-offs are embedded directly into the system logic. Risk controls operate autonomously, intervening even in the event of total connectivity or human outage.

04

Continuous Automated Monitoring

Our systems run continuously around the clock. Automated heartbeat signals and watchdog processes monitor system health in real time, triggering alerts instantly upon detecting anomalies.
