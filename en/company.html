// Company

JKHD builds quantitative systems — for its own use.

An independent company based in Schliengen, Germany. We develop our systems ourselves, run them ourselves and do not offer them for sale.

// Profile

Built out of what we needed ourselves.

JKHD develops and runs quantitative systems: programs that decide by fixed rules, tested in advance, not by feel. The systems work for us alone.

It did not begin with a business plan but with a need of our own. Anyone who wants to work in the markets by rules rather than by gut feeling needs tools that do not exist ready-made: data whose gaps are not quietly filled in. Statistics that are willing to say no. An emergency stop that triggers when nobody is watching. Because none of it could be bought, we built it. Out of it came our own system, which has been running and monitored ever since.

What JKHD does not do

We do not sell software, trading signals or advice. We provide no investment advice, no investment brokerage and no portfolio management, manage no third-party assets and hold no client money.

Exchange

We have nothing to sell — but we are open to professional exchange with other quants. Write to us via the contact page. Partners of JKHD sign in via partner access.

// Principles

How we work.

01

Everything in-house

Data processing, research, software, risk limits and operation are all done by us. We buy in no outside black box — which is why we can explain every line.

02

Statistics that may say no

Every idea is tested against chance before it is allowed to run. A rejected signal is not a setback for us but a result.

03

Limits before returns

Loss limits and an emergency stop are part of every system. The emergency stop triggers even when nobody is watching.

04

Monitored around the clock

Our systems run continuously and are watched all the time. They report anything unusual by themselves.

How we test and safeguard our systems is described on the pages Testing and Security.

// The founder

Who is behind it.

Julian Lukas Kaiser
Founder | System architecture & research

Julian Lukas Kaiser founded JKHD and runs the company. He is responsible for the architecture of the systems and for research — from data processing through the models and the risk limits to monitoring in continuous operation.

Background

After leaving school with a Hauptschulabschluss — the German general secondary school certificate — he completed an apprenticeship in the electrical trade. Alongside it, he taught himself quantitative financial market analysis and algorithm development. JKHD grew out of technical systems understanding and data-based analysis.
