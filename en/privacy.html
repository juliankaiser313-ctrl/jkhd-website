Principle

This website uses no cookies, no trackers and no analytics services. All fonts and content are loaded locally from this domain — no third-party content is fetched. Only your choice of appearance is stored on your device; see the following section.

Setting stored on your device

This website can be switched between a light and a dark appearance. If you operate that switch, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage, key jkhd-theme, value light or dark) so that the site greets you next time the way you left it.

This entry contains no personal data, is not evaluated and is not transmitted to us or to third parties — it does not leave your device. Because storage takes place solely in response to your own action and solely in order to provide the appearance you requested, it does not require consent under § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG; a consent banner is not needed for it. You can delete the entry at any time through your browser settings. As long as you do not operate the switch, nothing is stored — the site then follows your operating system setting.

Switching between German and English stores nothing at all: the language switch is an ordinary link to the corresponding page.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the site is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time of access and browser information in server logs. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and efficiently). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Enquiry form

The form on the contact page transmits nothing. From your entries it merely produces a message text and passes it to your own e-mail program or to your clipboard. There is no server that receives the entries, no storage and no disclosure to third parties. Only once you send the message yourself from your e-mail program do your details reach us — the following section then applies.

Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your details exclusively in order to deal with your enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR). The data is not passed on to third parties and is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for that purpose.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise them, please write to the e-mail address given above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — the competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.
